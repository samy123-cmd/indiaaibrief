## How to read conflicting market sizes

Analysts disagree because definitions disagree. Software-only genAI spend is not the same as IT/BPM AI-tagged exports, which is not the same as infra/GPU capex. When two slides conflict:

1. Name the definition on both slides.  
2. Prefer primary India publishers (NASSCOM, MeitY, NPCI) for India-specific claims.  
3. Use global indexes (Stanford HAI, OECD) for cross-country comparisons, not for India TAM.  
4. Never average incompatible series into one “consensus” number.
