Sarvam AI is betting on Indic models and products with IndiaAI Mission compute turning into possible **1–2% Centre equity**; Krutrim, India’s first GenAI unicorn, pivoted to **AI cloud**, reporting ~**₹300 Cr** FY26 revenue and first profit while pausing chips and foundation models. Choose by workload: models vs GPUs — not by who shouted “sovereign” louder in 2024.
</Answer>

Both companies sold “India’s AI.” Only one is still primarily selling models. If you are a founder writing an RFQ this quarter, nationalism is not a vendor-selection criterion. Unit economics and roadmap honesty are.

## What Changed

- Sarvam: ongoing ~**$300M** raise near **$1.5B** valuation; Mission CCDs may yield Centre **1–2%** stake ([news brief](/news/sarvam-ai-government-stake-convertible-debentures)).
- Krutrim: late-2025 realignment to **AI cloud**; ~**₹300 Cr** FY26 revenue; first net profit; chips/models de-prioritised ([pivot brief](/news/krutrim-pivot-cloud-services-india-ai)).
- Enterprise buyers now split “Indic model API” and “India GPU cloud” into different line items.

### Pricing and API costs

Neither publishes a stable public “₹ per 1M tokens” card that founders should trust for 12-month budgets. Practical approach:

1. Run a **72-hour bake-off** on your top 20 prompts (Hindi + English + code).
2. Measure **cost per successful task**, not sticker tokens.
3. Add **egress, fine-tune, and support** fees.
4. Require **India region** and log export in the order form.

Expect Indic-optimised APIs to win on quality-adjusted cost for Hindi/vernacular support workflows even when raw $/1M tokens look higher — test, don’t assume.

### Enterprise customers

- **Sarvam:** Banking and defence-adjacent narratives in press; treat logos as sales claims until you get reference calls.
- **Krutrim:** 25+ enterprises across telecom, BFSI, healthcare claimed; validate whether your workload resembles Ola-group transfer pricing or true third-party SaaS.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is better for Indic LLM APIs — Sarvam or Krutrim?

For active foundation-model and Indic product roadmaps, Sarvam is the clearer model partner in 2026. Krutrim has de-prioritised model building in favour of AI cloud — evaluate Krutrim primarily on GPU/cloud SLAs.

### Is Krutrim still India’s AI unicorn to partner with?

Krutrim remains a capitalised cloud player with ~₹300 Cr FY26 revenue and first profit, but its late-2025 pivot paused chip and foundation-model push. Partner for infrastructure, not for a frontier-model roadmap.

### Does government backing make Sarvam safer for enterprises?

IndiaAI Mission compute converting to a possible 1–2% Centre stake signals industrial-policy alignment — helpful for some PSU/defence-adjacent buyers, and an extra governance diligence item for others.

### Can I use both Sarvam and Krutrim?

Yes. Common pattern: Sarvam (or other Indic models) for language tasks + Krutrim or hyperscaler GPUs for training/inference hosting. Dual-source like you would any critical vendor.
