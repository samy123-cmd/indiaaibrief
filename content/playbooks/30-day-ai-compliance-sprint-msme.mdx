<Answer>
Indian MSMEs can reach RFP-ready AI compliance hygiene in 30 days: inventory personal data in training and inference, document lawful grounds, list subprocessors, ship immutable logs and human override for high-impact flows, and package a short annex for buyers. This is operational readiness — not a substitute for counsel on regulated deals.
</Answer>

You do not need a six-month “AI governance programme” to stop failing security questionnaires. You need a sprint with owners and artefacts. Use this beside the [DPDP training guide](/explains/dpdp-act-ai-training-data), [RFP questionnaire explainer](/explains/ai-rfp-dpdp-questionnaire-india), and [MeitY framework news](/news/meity-ai-governance-framework-2026).

## What Changed

- Enterprise buyers now expect **DPDP + AI oversight** evidence before pilots.
- MSMEs lose deals to **missing tables**, not missing ISO certificates.
- Chatbots and RAG on Indian customer data are the common blast radius — not giant foundation-model labs.
- Templates beat blank Notion pages: [AI Compliance Starter Kit](/kit/ai-compliance) (₹999) and [AI Readiness Audit](/audit) (₹4,999).
- Track evolving instruments on the [Policy Tracker](/policy).

## The Details

### Week 1 — Inventory and purpose (days 1–7)

**Owner:** Founder or Head of Product.

- List every AI feature in production or pilot (chatbot, lead score, OCR, coding assist).
- For each, mark personal data fields in prompts, indexes, fine-tunes, and logs.
- Write one purpose sentence per field; flag purpose creep.
- Draft or refresh customer-facing notice language covering model improvement and logging.

**Exit criteria:** One spreadsheet (or workspace board) with stage → fields → purpose → owner.

### Week 2 — Vendors and regions (days 8–14)

**Owner:** Engineering lead.

- Subprocessor table: model APIs, vector DB, observability, support tools, email.
- Region and transfer notes for each; India-hosting options for BFSI/health prospects.
- Contracts or DPAs: who deletes on request; who can train on your inputs (default: nobody).
- Kill public “paste production data into ChatGPT” habits — replace with redacted eval sets.

**Exit criteria:** Subprocessor table v1 + named deletion contact.

### Week 3 — Oversight and logs (days 15–21)

**Owner:** Eng + ops.

- Risk-tier features: low (FAQ bot) vs high (credit, hiring, medical triage).
- High-tier: human review path, override, and a tested “stop the model” step.
- Log fields (minimum): `model_version`, `prompt_hash`, `output_hash`, `reviewer_id`, `decision`, `ts_utc`.
- Incident stub: who gets paged if personal data leaks via model output.

**Exit criteria:** Runbook PDF/Markdown + sample log line in staging.

### Week 4 — RFP annex and dry run (days 22–30)

**Owner:** Founder + sales engineer.

- Assemble 4–6 page annex answering the [eight RFP questions](/explains/ai-rfp-dpdp-questionnaire-india).
- Dry-run with a friendly security contact or internal red team.
- Schedule quarterly review date; put it on the calendar.
- Optional: score gaps with an [AI Readiness Audit](/audit) before a bank pilot.

| Item | Low | High |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Internal time (5–15 person team) | ₹25,000 | ₹80,000 |
| Compliance kit templates | ₹999 | ₹999 |
| Readiness audit (optional) | — | ₹4,999 |

But here’s what others won’t tell you: **Week 2 is where sprints die.** Teams love Week 1 inventories and Week 3 architecture diagrams; they avoid calling vendors to ask “do you train on our prompts?” Put vendor emails on day 8, not day 28.

### Copy-ready 10-point checklist

- [ ] AI feature inventory complete
- [ ] Personal-data map for training + inference
- [ ] Purpose / ground documented per field
- [ ] Notices updated
- [ ] Subprocessor table with regions
- [ ] Deletion owner named
- [ ] High-tier HITL + stop procedure
- [ ] Log fields in staging
- [ ] Incident stub written
- [ ] RFP annex drafted and versioned

## What This Means for Indian MSME Founders and CTOs

1. **Time-box 30 days** with a single accountable owner — committee theatre fails.
2. **Buy templates** ([kit](/kit/ai-compliance)) instead of blank docs if your team is under 20 people.
3. **Do not wait for a final MeitY rulebook** to start DPDP hygiene buyers already require.
4. **Reuse this sprint** before every new AI feature launch — append rows, don’t restart.
5. **Escalate to counsel** for BFSI/health/government personal-data training — see [AI regulation guide](/explains/ai-regulation-india-business-guide).
6. **If you sell into fintech**, extend Week 3 with the [RBI model-risk checklist](/playbooks/rbi-ai-model-risk-compliance-checklist-fintech).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can an MSME finish AI compliance hygiene in 30 days?

Yes for operational hygiene — data map, notices, logging, vendor list, and HITL for high-tier flows. Regulated BFSI/health programmes and full legal opinions take longer and need counsel.

### What does a 30-day AI compliance sprint cost?

IndiaAIBrief desk estimate: ₹25,000–₹80,000 in internal time for a 5–15 person product team, plus ₹999–₹4,999 if you use our kit or readiness audit — before external counsel.

### Do we need MeitY registration to start the sprint?

No. Start with DPDP purpose mapping and oversight controls buyers already ask for. Track MeitY instruments on the [Policy Tracker](/policy) as they firm up.

### What is the minimum deliverable after 30 days?

A living data map, subprocessor table, log field list, human-override runbook for high-risk flows, and a 4–6 page RFP annex you can reuse.
