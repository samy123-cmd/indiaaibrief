IndiaAIBrief Press Kit
======================

Brand mark: "Signal Brief"
  Three dossier lines cut by a live red pulse — intelligence, not decoration.

Files
- logo.svg / logo-mark.svg     — primary vector mark (dark tile)
- logo-lockup.svg              — mark + wordmark (light backgrounds)
- logo-lockup-dark.svg         — mark + wordmark (dark backgrounds)
- logo.png / logo-192.png      — raster for schemas / PWA
- logo-mark-render.png         — high-res render for press
- color-guidelines.txt         — palette

Wordmark
  IndiaAIBrief — “AI” always in accent red (#DC2626 light / #F87171 dark)

Clear space
  Keep empty margin ≥ 1/4 mark height on all sides.

Do not
  Recolor the pulse to non-brand hues
  Add gradients, glow, 3D bevels, or neural-net clipart
  Stretch or rotate the mark
