# IndiaAIBrief Brand Colors

Light
- Background: #FAFAFA
- Surface: #FFFFFF
- Text Primary: #0A0A0A
- Text Secondary: #525252
- Accent (pulse / AI): #DC2626

Dark
- Background: #070707
- Surface: #0F0F0F
- Text Primary: #F0F0F0
- Accent (pulse / AI): #F87171

Mark
- Tile: #0A0A0A
- Brief lines: #F5F5F5
- Signal slash + live dot: accent red

Accent usage: CTAs, category labels, links, Signal Brief pulse.
Do not use purple gradients, neural-net clipart, or generic robot icons.
